INVASIVE SPECIES

European Green Crab

Carcinus maenas

Why it matters: where European green crabs become abundant, they can damage eelgrass and other seagrass beds, prey on shellfish, and threaten estuary habitat used by salmon and many other native species. Early detection and rapid removal while populations are still small gives managers the best chance to limit damage.

What does q mean?

q = P(detection in one check | occupied). It is not occupancy probability. The inference engine estimates q jointly with possible invasion extents.

You do not directly set q during a mission. Your operational control is effort: more checks reduce the chance of missing an occupied site.
